# Licence for the bundled recipe data

`src-tauri/data/seed.json` is built from the
[Wikibooks Cookbook](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Cookbook:Table_of_Contents),
which is licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

That licence covers the recipe names, ingredient wording, amounts and steps in
the seed file, and the attribution lines the app displays under each imported
recipe. If you publish a modified version of that data, share it under the same
licence and keep the attribution.

The Malay ingredient names in `src-tauri/data/aliases-ms.json` come from the
[PriceCatcher Item Lookup](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue/lookup_item) on
[data.gov.my](https://data.gov.my/), published by the Ministry of Domestic Trade
and the Department of Statistics Malaysia under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They let a search for
`beras`, `ayam` or `bawang merah` find the same recipes as `rice`, `chicken` or
`shallot`. Prices were not used, only the item names.

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers PantryPedia's own code, not the Wikibooks or
PriceCatcher data. Recipes you add in the app are yours.
